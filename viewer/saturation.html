BESS market saturation by bidding zone

Market conditions that drive battery revenues in each price area, and how much battery capacity is already installed relative to system size. Covers Sweden, the neighbours with a direct cable to Sweden, and the Baltics.

Negative-price hours

Hours per year with negative day-ahead prices (hourly average).

Average daily spread, 4 h (EUR/MWh)

Mean of the 4 highest hours minus the 4 lowest hours each day. Gross, before losses and fees.

Peak load (GW)

Highest hourly average load in the year.

Installed wind and solar as % of peak load

Installed capacity relative to the zone's highest hourly load. Above 100 % means wind and solar together could exceed the highest demand, a rough measure of how much flexibility the system needs.

No installed wind and solar capacity in the data for the selected zones and year. ENTSO-E may not report it for every zone; run the workflow manually with the proxy option to estimate it from peak output.

BESS as % of peak load

Solid bars: installed front-of-the-meter battery power. Outlined bars: batteries prequalified for reserve markets (a proxy; where available it can be higher or lower than installed).

No BESS capacity entered for the selected zones and year yet. Add MW and MWh per zone in data/saturation/bess_input.csv and re-run the workflow.

Weather-dependent power relative to demand

Wind and solar from ENTSO-E actual generation, relative to actual load, per bidding zone. It works also where installed capacity is not reported. Year and zones follow the controls above.

Share of annual demand (%)

Wind and solar energy divided by load energy.

Peak-hour penetration (%)

99th percentile of hourly wind and solar divided by load. Above 100 % means the zone regularly has surplus to export or store.

Daily residual-load swing (% of peak load)

Average of the highest minus lowest residual load each day, divided by peak load: how much flexibility the zone needs every day.

Fast ramps (% of peak load per 3 h)

99th percentile of 3-hour changes in residual load divided by peak load.

Prequalified batteries in the Nordic reserve markets

MW per country on 1 January 2025 (Nordic TSOs, April 2025). The TSOs report by country, not by bidding zone. A battery can be prequalified for several products, so the rows cannot be added together.

Sweden: batteries in the reserve markets

Volumes prequalified by Svenska kraftnät, national, 1 January each year and 1 October 2026. Prequalified is not the same as installed or bidding: one battery can be prequalified for several products at once.

Battery storage prequalified (MW)

Technology category "Energilager" only. Combinations with hydro or wind are not included.

Battery share of all prequalified volume (%)

Batteries relative to all technologies, per product. A high share means the market is already battery-driven.

Prequalified volume by bidding zone, all technologies (MW)

Sweden: FCR procurement (Svenska kraftnät Mimer)

What Svenska kraftnät actually buys, and at what price: hourly marginal prices and procured volumes, averaged per month. The latest month is month-to-date.

FCR marginal price (EUR/MW)

Monthly average of hourly marginal prices. Falling prices while battery volume grows point to saturation.

Battery-prequalified volume / procured volume

Prequalified battery MW divided by average procured MW in SE1-SE4 that month. Above 1 means batteries alone could cover the whole need.

Sweden: aFRR and mFRR capacity markets (Svenska kraftnät Data Service)

Volume-weighted marginal prices and procured capacity, monthly. Svenska kraftnät only publishes hours with procurement in Sweden, so averages cover published hours, not every hour of the month.

aFRR and mFRR capacity price (EUR/MW)

Volume-weighted monthly average of marginal prices.

Battery-prequalified volume / procured volume

Prequalified battery MW divided by the average procured MW per published hour that month.

Spread over time (4 h, EUR/MWh)

Same scale for all selected zones. The current year is year-to-date.

Data

Source: ENTSO-E Transparency Platform (load, day-ahead prices, installed capacity). Installed BESS from TSO and market reports entered manually. Spreads are gross and use hourly averages; partial years are year-to-date. Direct cable to Sweden: NO1, NO3, NO4, FI, DK1, DK2, DE-LU, PL, LT. EE and LV are included as part of the Baltics but connect to Sweden indirectly.
